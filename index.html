    About the metrics

    IoU = area(isochrone ∩ GHSL) / area(isochrone ∪ GHSL); 1 means identical shapes. GHSL covered = share of the GHSL area inside the isochrone. Outside GHSL = share of the isochrone area beyond the GHSL boundary. Areas are geodesic (Turf.js). Isochrones: HERE Isoline Routing v8. Free flow ignores traffic. The weekday times use HERE's typical traffic for that hour on the next Tuesday, in the city's local time. To the centre computes a reverse isochrone: everywhere you could leave from and still arrive at the origin within the time. Traffic only applies to car and scooter. GHSL polygons simplified at 100 m.
